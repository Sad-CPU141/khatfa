# Media credits

Built-in pictures and sounds used by Khatfa's picture and sound questions. Files have neutral (hash) names so they don't give answers away; `src/bank/02-media.js` maps them. Regenerate with `node tools/media.js <path to the CC0 sound collection>`.

| What | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Flags | [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) by Panayiotis Lipiridis | MIT (`licenses/flag-icons-MIT.txt`). National flags themselves are not copyrighted. |
| Country outlines and highlighted maps | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:50m / 1:110m, via [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) (Mike Bostock), drawn with d3-geo | Natural Earth: public domain. world-atlas: ISC (`licenses/world-atlas-ISC.txt`) |
| Saxophone, steelpan, 8-bit, pizzicato jingles; dice | [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) Music Jingles and Casino Audio | CC0 (`licenses/kenney-*.txt`) |
| Cat, keyboard typing, cello, slide whistle, record crackle, Rubik's Cube | Ben Burnes / [Abstraction](https://abstractionmusic.com) sample packs | CC0 (`licenses/ben-burnes-cc0-packs.txt`) |
| Morse SOS, dial tone, siren (Doppler), beats, 440 Hz, heartbeat, clock, mosquito, sonar, Shepard tone | Generated by `tools/media.js` (no source material) | Part of Khatfa |
| Emoji puzzles | Shown with the device's own emoji font; no image files | — |

The CC0 recordings were taken from the collection at github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds, using only packs that include their own CC0 notice (copied into `licenses/`).

No video is stored. Clip questions embed official YouTube uploads with YouTube's own player.
